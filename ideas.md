One app for everything students need during their university day: smart timetables, room navigation, deadline reminders, study-group matching, campus announcements and available study spaces.

## 2. Second-hand Study Materials Platform -- Taru
A marketplace where students can buy, sell or exchange used study materials, textbooks and lecture notes.

## 3. Student Odd-job Platform -- Cey
A platform connecting students with people or businesses looking for help with small jobs and student work.

## 4. University Supercomputer -- Taru
A shared computing network that uses students' available computing resources to create a university supercomputer.

## 5. University Dating App -- Taru
A dating app specifically for university students.

## 6. AI Video Checker -- Cey
A tool that checks whether a video is AI-generated or authentic, helping people identify potentially misleading content on social media.

## 7. Self-Trading AI Bot -- Cey
An AI bot that trades automatically, aiming to generate enough money to cover its own subscription costs and make a profit.

## 8. Puzzle-Based Alarm -- Cey / Phone Unlock -- Taru
An alarm that only turns off after the user solves a puzzle, or a phone-unlocking mechanism that requires completing a puzzle.

## 9. Life Micromanagement App -- Taru
An app that tracks a wide range of personal data, activities and habits to help users manage and monitor their lives.

## 10. Series & Movie Tracker -- Wiebe
An app combining watch-progress tracking, reviews, ratings and community forums, inspired by platforms such as IMDb and MyAnimeList.

## 12. Steam-Like Store for 18+ Games -- Taru

## 14. Indoor Navigation -- Taru
A navigation system that helps people find their way inside buildings, such as universities, shopping centres or large public spaces.

## 15. Late-Night Uber -- Taru
A ride service focused on helping people get home safely late at night.
